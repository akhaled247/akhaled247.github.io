# ITKAN's Mantik platform reaches 8 countries

**Alternate headline (if editor prefers):** From Plano to the global stage: ITKAN students share a free programming curriculum with the wider FRC community

---

**Category:** Community  
**By:** Abdullah Khaled  
**Date:** [Publication date TBD]

---

What began inside the ITKAN Innovation Hub is now reaching far beyond North Texas. **Mantik**, a free programming curriculum built by ITKAN students, has become one of the network's most visible contributions to the wider FIRST community—used by **more than 30 ITKAN students** locally and discovered by teams across **eight countries** since its launch in **June 2025**. Led by ITKAN programming student **Abdullah Khaled**, the project reflects a growing tradition at ITKAN: students do not only compete at the highest levels of FRC and FTC—they build resources that help others do the same.

Available at **[mantik.netlify.app](https://mantik.netlify.app/)**, Mantik offers a single home for students learning to program competition robots—from their first lessons in Java through the skills they need to contribute on an FRC or FTC team. Written guides, video tutorials, and interactive practice tools sit side by side so learners can move at their own pace, whether they are sitting in the Hub in Plano or opening the site from halfway around the world. The platform is free, open, and designed to be shared: any team, anywhere, can use it without cost.

That spirit of generosity is what sets ITKAN apart. In a community where strong teams often guard their advantages, ITKAN students chose to publish what they learned and make it available to everyone. Mantik carries the same ethos that has defined the network—from state championships and World Championship runs to partnerships that stretch from Texas to Saudi Arabia. It is proof that ITKAN's influence is not limited to the teams that wear its name on their bumpers.

The broader FRC community has taken notice. Since June 2025, Mantik has appeared in search results more than **3,350 times** on Google, with **50+ teams and students clicking through** to explore the curriculum. On **[Chief Delphi](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/resource-mantik-the-15th-competing-standard/522085)**—the forum where experienced FRC programmers gather to share resources—ITKAN's project has drawn **88 clicks** from teams seeking better ways to onboard new programmers. Those numbers tell a simple story: ITKAN is helping shape how the next generation of FIRST programmers learn, not only inside its own walls, but across the global FRC ecosystem.

Mantik is one chapter in a larger ITKAN story—of students who build, mentor, and give back. As Summer Training and recruitment for the 2026–2027 season continue at the Innovation Hub, the platform stands as a reminder that ITKAN's reach extends well beyond any single event or award. Teams everywhere are invited to explore **[mantik.netlify.app](https://mantik.netlify.app/)**, share it with their programmers, and join the growing community of students learning through a resource born at ITKAN.

Learn more about the project at **[aakhaled.com/personal/mantik/](https://www.aakhaled.com/personal/mantik/)**.
