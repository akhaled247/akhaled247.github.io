Output structure

Plain English: what this school emphasizes for someone like me
Academic readiness, community/culture, initiative, collaboration, depth vs breadth, risk/iteration, and anything distinctive to this place (not generic “college tips”).

Qualities the school likely values (grouped)
For each bucket, give bullets the admissions lens would plausibly care about:

Academic preparation (for their expectations, not “more APs”)
Motivation / fit (why this school and this path now)
Evidence of impact (proof beyond aspirations: outcomes, metrics, iteration, leadership)
Communication & application mechanics (essay length, typical themes if known, interview vibe if applicable)
Field-specific competencies (what “strong preparation” looks like for my intended focus)
Under each bucket, add: Evidence I can draw from my materials (cite files/projects explicitly).
Any gap in my story—how to frame it honestly
e.g. limited formal research, no flagship awards, narrow extracurricular menu—translate into what I did build, measure, and learn instead of apologizing.

Style

Direct, specific, and usable while drafting applications—no platitudes.
When uncertain about a school-specific fact, label it as inference and suggest what to confirm on the official site.
Close with one concrete next step tailored to my application (e.g. “pick four activities and map each to the best supplemental prompt”) only if my materials list what I’m actually submitting.
