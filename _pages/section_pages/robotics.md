I have competed with FIRST Robotics from freshman to senior year, building my technical skillset in mechanical, electrical, and software engineering. Moreover, I have developed many soft skills, including public speaking, budgeting, marketing, fundraising, and more from my time in FIRST, which have given me the foundation to build not only robots, but robotics programs.

    <h3 style="margin: 0; padding; 0;">FIRST Tech Challenge: 2025-2026</h3>

    The 2025-2026 Season of FTC, DECODE, consisted of two robots on one alliance scoring up to three ARTIFACTS into a GOAL for the duration of the match. During this season, our team 33791 Wolverine Robotics had a rocky start, forming our team a mere 11 days before competition. However, between administrative support and strong team determination, we competed at an exceedingly high level, winning the Dallas Semi-Regional and placing third at the UIL State Championship! As the main programmer on the team, I oversaw our software development on the robot. I guided other software members through creating and refining our subsystems while developing more complex functions including auto-align, distance-based scoring, and autonomous routines. By the end of the season, we placed in the top 10% of all terms worldwide performance-wise and hope to carry that momentum into our second season!

This season significantly contributed to my development as a programmer, as my elevated role as Software Lead encouraged me to understand motion profiling, autonomous routines, and pose estimation at a much deeper level. Though we ultimately did not make it to the world championship, the skills our team learned almost certainly contributed to our success in REBUILT.
